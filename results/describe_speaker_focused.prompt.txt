### Task Overview
You will be given a video clip, with audio, with one main speaker.
### Task Guidelines
- Without using any adjectives or emotions, describe the changes in the facial features
(eyebrows, forehead, eyes, nose, cheeks, mouth and lips) and the body language (arms,
hands, legs, torso) of the main speaker. Do not interpret what they mean.
- Quote what the speaker says.
- Output only these two parts.
### Response Format
Speech: "<the speaker's exact words>"
Description: <the changes in the speaker's face and body, and what else happens in the clip>
