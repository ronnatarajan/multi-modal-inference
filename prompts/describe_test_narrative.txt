### Task Overview
You are given a short video clip from a TV show, with audio. There is one main speaker.
Describe what happens in the clip, focusing on the main speaker.
### Task Guidelines
- Decide who the main speaker is from the mouth movements of the people in the foreground.
- Without using any adjectives or emotions, describe the changes in the facial features
(eyebrows, forehead, eyes, nose, cheeks, mouth and lips) and the body language (arms,
hands, legs, torso) of the main speaker. Do not interpret what they mean.
- Describe what else happens in the clip.
- Quote what the speaker says.
- Output only these two parts.
### Response Format
Speech: "<the speaker's exact words>"
Description: <what happens in the clip>
